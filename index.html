1. Who we are

TrustJob ("the app", "we", "us") is an Android application developed and published by an independent developer. TrustJob aggregates publicly available job listings from third-party sources and applies a transparent, on-device trust analysis to help users identify potentially misleading or low-quality listings.

The app is distributed exclusively through Google Play. This policy explains what data TrustJob does and does not handle, and how that may change if we introduce new features in the future.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Personal information

We do not collect, request, or store any personal information. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your location or IP address
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, or files
  • Your device identifiers, advertising ID, or IMEI
  • Your search queries, viewed listings, or application history
  • Any data derived from your use of the app

2.2 Analytics and tracking

TrustJob contains no analytics, no tracking SDKs, no advertising networks, and no crash reporting services. We do not know how many users have installed the app, which features they use, or where they are located. This is intentional and permanent.

2.3 Permissions

TrustJob requests only the network permission required to fetch job listings from public APIs. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

3. Information stored on your device

TrustJob stores the following data locally on your device using Android's standard mechanisms (SharedPreferences and Room database). This data is:

  • Never transmitted to us or to any third party
  • Only accessible to the TrustJob app on your device
  • Fully removable by you at any time through the Settings screen or by uninstalling the app

3.1 What is stored

  • Saved jobs — listings you have explicitly saved, including title, company, description, and trust analysis
  • Tracked applications — jobs you have marked as applied to, plus their status (Applied, Interviewing, Offer, Rejected)
  • Seen jobs — a list of listings you have opened, used to power the "hide seen" filter
  • App settings — your preferences for theme, language, currency, filters, and display options
  • Repost fingerprints — anonymous cryptographic hashes used to detect duplicate or reposted listings
  • Company stability cache — publicly available information about companies, cached locally to avoid repeated lookups

3.2 Clearing your data

You can clear any portion of this data at any time:

  • Open Settings → Data & Storage to clear individual categories
  • Use Android's Settings → Apps → TrustJob → Storage → Clear data to erase everything
  • Uninstall the app to remove all stored data

4. Job listings from third-party sources

TrustJob retrieves job listings from publicly accessible, keyless job boards and APIs. When you open the app, it makes direct requests from your device to these sources. The sources receive only the standard technical information required by any HTTP request (such as your IP address and a generic user agent). They do not receive any information about you, your saved jobs, or your activity in the app.

Current sources:

  • Himalayas (himalayas.app)
  • Jobicy (jobicy.com)
  • RemoteOK (remoteok.com)
  • Remotive (remotive.com)
  • Arbeitnow (arbeitnow.com)
  • Get on Board (getonbrd.com)
  • freehire.me
  • Vacantes Digitales (vacantesdigitales.com)

For company stability analysis, TrustJob also queries the following publicly available sources:

  • U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission EDGAR (sec.gov, data.sec.gov)
  • aiLayoffs.org

Each of these sources has its own privacy policy, which governs their handling of standard HTTP request data. We encourage you to review them if you have concerns.

4.1 Applying to a job

When you tap "Apply Now", TrustJob opens the original job listing in your device's browser or the source app. From that point, you are subject to that source's terms and privacy policy. TrustJob does not transmit any data on your behalf during this transition.

5. Third-party services

TrustJob currently integrates with no third-party analytics, advertising, authentication, or cloud services. The only external communication is direct HTTP requests to the job sources listed above for the purpose of retrieving publicly available listings.

6. Future account features

TrustJob does not currently offer user accounts, cloud backup, or cross-device synchronisation. If we introduce these features in a future release, they will:

  • Be optional. The app will continue to work fully without any account.
  • Require explicit consent. You will be asked to opt in before any account is created or any data is uploaded.
  • Be described in an updated version of this policy. We will publish a new effective date and notify users through the app and the Play Store listing before the feature is enabled.
  • Use established providers. If we add cloud backup, it will use Google services (such as Google Sign-In and Google Drive app data folder), which are governed by Google's own privacy policy.
  • Store the same data you already have on-device — saved jobs, tracked applications, and settings. Nothing additional will be collected.

Until such features are released and this policy is updated, TrustJob remains a fully offline, account-free app.

7. Your rights

7.1 Under GDPR (EU/EEA/UK users)

Because TrustJob does not collect personal data, most GDPR rights are automatically satisfied. If we ever do collect personal data, you will have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of that data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Request data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

7.2 Under CCPA/CPRA (California users)

TrustJob does not sell, share, or disclose personal information. You have the right to know what personal information is collected (none), to request deletion (not applicable), and to opt out of sale (not applicable — we do not sell data).

7.3 Under other jurisdictions

If your jurisdiction grants additional privacy rights, those rights apply. Because we do not collect data, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete. To exercise any right, contact us using the details in section 12.

8. Children's privacy

TrustJob is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect any data from children. Because the app collects no personal information whatsoever, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent regulations by design.

If you believe a child has used the app in a manner that concerns you, contact us and we will investigate. Note that, since we have no backend and collect no data, there is nothing to remove from our side.

9. Security

All data stored by TrustJob remains on your device and is protected by Android's standard app sandboxing and, where enabled, device-level encryption. Because we operate no server, there is no database to breach and no data to leak on our side.

Communication with job sources uses HTTPS. TrustJob does not accept user credentials, does not transmit authentication tokens, and does not expose an attack surface for credential theft.

10. International users

TrustJob can be installed on any Android device worldwide. Because the app does not transmit personal data to us, there is no cross-border data transfer to disclose. Direct requests to job sources go from your device to those sources' servers; their own privacy policies govern that communication.
