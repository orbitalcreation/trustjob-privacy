1. Who we are

TrustJob ("the app", "we", "us") is an Android application developed and published by an independent developer. TrustJob aggregates publicly available job listings from third-party sources and applies a transparent, on-device trust analysis to help users identify potentially misleading or low-quality listings.

The app is distributed exclusively through Google Play. This policy explains what data TrustJob does and does not handle, and how that may change if we introduce new features in the future.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Personal information

We do not collect, request, or store any personal information. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your location or IP address (beyond what is required to deliver ads, see section 6)
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, or files
  • Your search queries, viewed listings, or application history

2.2 Analytics and tracking

TrustJob contains no analytics, no crash reporting, and no tracking SDKs of our own. The only third-party SDKs are Google AdMob (for ads) and Google Play Billing (for purchases). Both are described in section 6.

2.3 Permissions

TrustJob requests only the network permission required to fetch job listings and to load ads. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

3. Information stored on your device

TrustJob stores the following data locally on your device using Android's standard mechanisms. This data is:

  • Never transmitted to us or to any third party
  • Only accessible to the TrustJob app on your device
  • Fully removable by you at any time through the Settings screen or by uninstalling the app

3.1 What is stored

  • Saved jobs — listings you have explicitly saved, including title, company, description, and trust analysis
  • Tracked applications — jobs you have marked as applied to, plus their status
  • Seen jobs — a list of listings you have opened, used to power the "hide seen" filter
  • App settings — your preferences for theme, language, currency, filters, and display options
  • Repost fingerprints — anonymous cryptographic hashes used to detect duplicate or reposted listings
  • Company stability cache — publicly available information about companies, cached locally to avoid repeated lookups

3.2 Clearing your data

You can clear any portion of this data at any time:

  • Open Settings → Data & Storage to clear individual categories
  • Use Android's Settings → Apps → TrustJob → Storage → Clear data to erase everything
  • Uninstall the app to remove all stored data

4. Job listings from third-party sources

TrustJob retrieves job listings from publicly accessible, keyless job boards and APIs. When you open the app, it makes direct requests from your device to these sources. The sources receive only the standard technical information required by any HTTP request. They do not receive any information about you, your saved jobs, or your activity in the app.

Current sources:

  • Himalayas (himalayas.app)
  • Jobicy (jobicy.com)
  • RemoteOK (remoteok.com)
  • Remotive (remotive.com)
  • Arbeitnow (arbeitnow.com)
  • Get on Board (getonbrd.com)
  • freehire.me
  • Vacantes Digitales (vacantesdigitales.com)

For company stability analysis, TrustJob also queries:

  • U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission EDGAR (sec.gov, data.sec.gov)
  • aiLayoffs.org

Each source has its own privacy policy, which governs their handling of standard HTTP request data.

4.1 Applying to a job

When you tap "Apply Now", TrustJob opens the original job listing in your device's browser or the source app. From that point, you are subject to that source's terms and privacy policy. TrustJob does not transmit any data on your behalf during this transition.

5. Third-party services

TrustJob integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — serves the ads you see in the app (see section 6)
  • Google Play Billing — processes the one-time Supporter pack purchase

The app does not use any other analytics, advertising, authentication, or cloud services.

6. Advertising and purchases

6.1 Ads

TrustJob displays interstitial advertisements served by Google AdMob. Ads appear no more than once every four job listings you open. We use non-personalized ads only — AdMob does not build a profile of you based on your activity across other apps or websites.

Even with non-personalized ads, Google may still receive:

  • A device or advertising identifier
  • Your approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interaction events (impression, click, dismissal)
  • General device and OS information

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. See Google's advertising privacy notice at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

6.2 Supporter pack

TrustJob offers a one-time in-app purchase called the Supporter pack. It permanently removes all ads and unlocks the "Hide propaganda" setting.

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your name, email, payment method, or any other personal information. We only receive a confirmation that the purchase was completed, which is stored locally on your device so the app knows to skip ads.

Refunds are handled by Google Play according to their refund policy.

6.3 How to opt out of ads

You can permanently disable all advertising in TrustJob by purchasing the Supporter pack from the app's top bar.

7. Future account features

TrustJob does not currently offer user accounts, cloud backup, or cross-device synchronisation. If we introduce these features in a future release, they will:

  • Be optional. The app will continue to work fully without any account.
  • Require explicit consent. You will be asked to opt in before any account is created or any data is uploaded.
  • Be described in an updated version of this policy. We will publish a new effective date and notify users through the app and the Play Store listing before the feature is enabled.
  • Use established providers. If we add cloud backup, it will use Google services governed by Google's own privacy policy.

8. Your rights

8.1 Under GDPR (EU/EEA/UK users)

Where TrustJob handles personal data at all, it does so through Google AdMob (for advertising) and Google Play Billing (for purchases). You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data held about you
  • Request correction or deletion of that data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Request data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights against Google, see Google's privacy controls.

8.2 Under CCPA/CPRA (California users)

TrustJob does not sell personal information. Advertising is delivered through Google AdMob with non-personalized ads only. You may opt out of all advertising by purchasing the Supporter pack.

9. Children's privacy

TrustJob is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect any data from children. The app's job listings are employment advertisements, which are not appropriate for children.

10. Security

All locally stored data remains on your device and is protected by Android's app sandboxing and, where enabled, device-level encryption. Communication with job sources and with Google's ad and billing services uses HTTPS.

We do not accept user credentials, we do not transmit authentication tokens, and we do not operate a server that could be breached.

11. International users

TrustJob can be installed on any Android device worldwide. The only cross-border data transfer is between your device and Google's AdMob and Play Billing services, governed by Google's privacy policy.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when:

  • We add a new feature that changes what data is handled
  • We add or remove a third-party job source or integration
  • Legal or regulatory requirements change

When we update the policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and describe the changes in a visible notice within the app before the change takes effect if the change materially affects how data is handled.
